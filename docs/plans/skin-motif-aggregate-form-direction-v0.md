# SKIN Motif Aggregate Form Direction v0

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: Concept / roadmap addendum

## Purpose

This document records a direction that became clearer during the first physical SKIN prints and the ongoing Support Physical Gate.

The important shift is that the final artwork should not be understood only as a pre-existing Base Shape with decorative Motifs attached to its surface.

A stronger long-term direction is:

```text
Authored Motifs
+
Connections / Graph
+
Density variation
+
Permanent Internal Web
↓
The larger form emerges from the aggregate
```

The visible whole should increasingly be produced by the collection and relationship of authored parts themselves.

This is a roadmap clarification, not an instruction to rewrite Golden LUNA now.

- `docs/plans/skin-golden-rebuild-roadmap-v1.md`

## 1. Observation

The current synthetic test object has been useful because its strong verticality exposes support instability, long unbraced runs, bootstrap failure, and contact problems clearly.

The first physical print also showed that the upper internal structure survived better than expected after removable support removal, while more failures appeared in the lower / middle-lower region.

This makes the current object valuable as a **Synthetic Vertical Stress Fixture**.

However, it is not representative enough of the author's intended form language.

The actual artwork direction is expected to contain more:

- asymmetry
- diagonal growth
- uneven heights
- lateral extension
- cavities
- near and distant motif relationships
- irregular junctions
- non-vertical routing
- local density changes

Therefore SKIN must not become optimized only for a vertically biased synthetic fixture.

---

## 2. Motif aggregate as form

The desired artwork direction is not simply:

```text
Base
↓
Surface Pattern attached to Base
```

The stronger direction is:

```text
Base / Field / Envelope guide
        ↓
many authored Motifs
        ↓
placement + orientation + scale + density
        ↓
connections / structural Graph
        ↓
Permanent Web / mutual support
        ↓
aggregate artwork
        ↓
Derived overall Envelope
```

The Base may remain useful, but its role can gradually change from "the final visible object" toward:

- spatial guide
- field / envelope constraint
- placement domain
- density guide
- collision / containment reference

The Motifs, their connections, and the permanent structural relationships should be allowed to become the actual artwork.

The overall form can then be understood as a **Derived Envelope** of the authored aggregate rather than a shell that must always remain visually dominant.

---

## 3. Why HANA matters

This direction strengthens the role of HANA inside the SKIN ecosystem.

HANA should remain an independent 3D Drawing Instrument, but it can provide authored Motifs / Strokes for SKIN.

Preferred relationship:

```text
HANA
Raw Gesture
↓
Control Stroke
↓
Portable Motif Definition
        ↓
SKIN
Motif aggregation
↓
Graph / mutual support
↓
Physical realization
```

The important point is that the parts should retain author-originated differences in gesture, hesitation, unevenness, and proportion.

Do not replace that with generic AI randomness or a purely procedural "organic" style.

The long-term value is precisely the combination:

```text
Human-authored parts
+
computationally managed multiplicity
+
structural viability
```

The quantity and spatial complexity may exceed what can be made directly by hand, while the individual forms can still retain authorship.

---

## 4. Why this is not "AI makes the sculpture"

The system should not simply ask an AI generator to produce a complex organic object and then print it.

The intended division is closer to:

```text
Author / HANA
creates meaningful Motifs and gestures

SKIN
places, relates, connects, diagnoses, and makes them viable

ART
studies whether the resulting whole has the desired visual / conceptual character

Physical printing
provides evidence that feeds back into structure and authoring
```

This is important because the target work depends on both:

- a degree of multiplicity and structural coordination that is difficult to make directly by hand
- a degree of authored specificity that is difficult to preserve through generic automatic generation alone

---

## 5. Relationship to ART

ART already points toward useful principles:

- HAND REMAINS
- SUPPORT BECOMES FORM
- MUTUAL SUPPORT
- PERMANENT / CHANGING
- motif remains visible
- density breathes
- internal structure may be visible

The Motif Aggregate direction should therefore be evaluated bidirectionally with ART.

ART can ask:

- does the aggregate read as one whole without losing the individual Motifs?
- is the density too uniform?
- do connections become visually meaningful rather than hidden infrastructure?
- does the larger form emerge from the parts rather than looking like a decorated shell?
- do gaps, light, and shadow produce the intended environmental quality?

Useful visual discoveries can then return to SKIN as explicit authoring or structural research questions.

---

## 6. Relationship to Support and Permanent Web

Recent removable-support work is not separate from this direction.

Physical support testing is revealing structural principles that may later inform Permanent Web design:

```text
Removable Support evidence
- maximum viable unbraced length
- bootstrap stability
- mutual bracing
- useful junction spacing
- single-point dependency
- distributed contact
- member diameter
- branch angle
- shared trunk / branching opportunities

        ↓ principles / evidence only

Permanent Web
- mutual support
- branch / junction logic
- distributed load paths
- early network formation
- permanent connection to Motifs / artwork
```

Do not copy the Removable Support algorithm directly into Permanent Web.

The semantics remain different:

```text
Removable Support
= temporary fabrication aid
= must remain removable

Permanent Web
= artwork structure
= should fuse reliably and remain in the work
```

But the physical evidence can be shared.

---

## 7. Shared trunk / branching direction

A related Support idea should be retained as future evidence:

```text
one stable trunk
↓
branch junction
↙          ↘
target A    target B
```

This can potentially reduce:

- large numbers of independent long support shafts
- support-support route interference
- lower bootstrap instability
- inability to reach multiple targets through a narrow corridor

This is not part of the current Print #2 comparison; Print #2 should continue to isolate the effect of mutual bracing.

Branched Support belongs to a later Support candidate so its effect can be measured separately.

The same branch / junction / shared-path idea may later become relevant to Permanent Web, but again only at the level of structural principle unless explicitly migrated.

## 8. Fixture strategy

From this point, use at least three levels of geometry evidence.

### Fixture A — Synthetic Vertical Stress Fixture

The current GPT-generated / synthetic object.

Purpose:

- regression
- Support physics
- bootstrap / long-member stress
- deterministic comparison

Keep it permanently as a difficult engineering fixture.

### Fixture B — Author Organic Fixture

A small object created by the author rather than generated primarily by GPT.

It should contain enough of the real design language to expose different problems, for example:

- 10–30 authored Motifs
- asymmetry
- diagonal stems / branches
- uneven height
- lateral spread
- cavities
- close and separated Motifs
- irregular junctions
- non-vertical support demand

It does not need to be the final Bouquet.

Purpose:

- test whether SKIN solves the author's geometry rather than only its own synthetic fixture
- expose support-routing and Web problems hidden by vertical test geometry
- test whether Motif aggregation itself can create a convincing small whole

### Fixture C — Production Bouquet

The actual artwork candidate.

Do not use the full production Bouquet as the first place to discover every structural rule.

## 9. Roadmap comparison

This direction is compatible with the existing Golden / Rebuild roadmap, but it changes the interpretation of several steps.

### Existing roadmap idea

```text
Base
↓
Surface Pattern
↓
Internal Graph / Reinforcement / DryWeb
↓
Bouquet
```

### Clarified long-term interpretation

```text
Base / Field / Envelope Guide
↓
Author Motifs
↓
Motif Aggregate
↓
Connections / Structural Graph
↓
Permanent Web
↓
Derived overall form / Envelope
↓
Residual Removable Support
```

Do not remove the existing Base / Surface Pattern pipeline from Golden merely because this longer-term direction exists.

Golden should continue to finish and physically validate the current work.

Rebuild is the more appropriate place for later non-destructive representations where Base, Motif Aggregate, Graph, and Derived Envelope can coexist explicitly.

## 10. Roadmap adjustment after the current Support Gate

The immediate Print #2 experiment remains unchanged.

Current order:

```text
Print #1 complete
↓
Support Physical Feedback v1
↓
Print #2 with same BODY / Permanent Structure
↓
Physical comparison
```

After Print #2, do not continue only on the Synthetic Vertical Stress Fixture.

Preferred next sequence:

```text
Print #2 physical comparison
↓
freeze Support findings as physical evidence
↓
introduce Author Organic Fixture
↓
validate Support on Synthetic + Author fixtures
↓
identify true BODY / Permanent Structure failures
↓
Permanent Web development
↕
Support / BODY / Web physical feedback loop
↓
Motif Aggregate study
↓
Production Bouquet
```

Support does not need to become theoretically perfect before Web begins.

It needs to become reliable enough that Support failure is no longer confused with BODY / Web failure.

After that point, Support, BODY, and Permanent Web may improve iteratively from physical evidence.

## 11. Golden / Rebuild boundary

### Golden LUNA

Golden may implement only what is needed to finish the current artwork and validate the Motif Aggregate direction physically.

Examples:

- current Motif authoring / import
- support stability
- current Permanent Reinforcement
- production-useful DryWeb / Permanent Web
- Author Organic Fixture
- physical Bouquet integration

Do not redesign the entire graph architecture inside Golden.

### Rebuild LUNA

Rebuild should eventually provide the cleaner architectural model for:

- Motif Artifact / authored motif identity
- Graph layers / provenance
- non-destructive Base / Aggregate / Derived Envelope separation
- explicit Control Graph / Shape Influence
- editable Motif-to-Graph relationships
- stable identity across aggregation and structural transforms

This direction should initially remain OBSERVE / SHADOW until the physical and authoring behavior is understood.

---

## 12. Important non-goals

This document does not authorize:

- immediate removal of Base Shape
- immediate replacement of Surface Pattern
- automatic AI-generated final artwork
- random motif variation as a substitute for authorship
- forcing every Motif into one graph topology
- making Removable Support permanent
- rewriting Golden during the current Print #2 gate
- changing FKEI schema now
- starting Graph → Shape production adoption now

---

## Guiding principle

The stronger artwork direction is:

> The whole should increasingly emerge from many authored parts and the relationships that make them physically possible.

Or, operationally:

```text
Author the parts.
Let computation coordinate the multiplicity.
Let structure make the aggregate viable.
Let the aggregate become the form.
```
