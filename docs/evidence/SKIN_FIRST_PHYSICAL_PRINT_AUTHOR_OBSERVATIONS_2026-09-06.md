### 4. Localized durability weakness

During casual play/handling, one terminal appendage/subgraph detached.

Author observation:

- the detached region appeared to be connected to the main artwork by essentially one narrow connection;
- the detached subgraph itself remained substantially coherent;
- failure therefore appears localized at the root connection rather than indicating global BODY collapse;
- an opposite-side / mirrored analogue is suspected to have the same weakness and was intentionally left intact as a comparison case.

Interpretation:

`single-attachment appendage durability = FAIL / LOCALIZED`

This justifies bridge / articulation / one-attachment graph auditing before any reinforcement change.

### 5. Shape regime likely contributed strongly to success

Author observation after handling the printed object:

- the printed shape was relatively straight / vertically organized;
- this likely contributed materially to successful printing and handling;
- the success should not be generalized automatically to strongly overhanging shapes.

Interpretation:

The First Physical Gate primarily supports a **near-vertical / linear shape regime**.

For an Usagi-like shape with stronger lateral projection / overhang, expect a different physical regime:

- more and longer overhang demand;
- larger horizontal support spans;
- greater print-time wobble / leverage at protruding regions;
- larger bending moments at Permanent Structure connections after printing;
- potentially more severe expression of single-attachment weaknesses.

Therefore Usagi + V6 parameter-locked validation should be treated as a new overhang/generalization test, not a simple repetition of the successful first print.

## Evidence boundary

### Supported by this first print

- full-height completion under the accepted combined support workflow;
- removable-support removal without global artwork collapse;
- visible retained Air / Void after removal;
- basic handling survival of most of the structure;
- one concrete localized single-attachment failure mode;
- successful behavior in the observed near-vertical / linear shape regime.

### Not yet proven

- SKIN-support-alone full printability;
- durability margin under repeated or deliberate load;
- robustness of every bridge / articulation / single-attachment region;
- behavior in strong-overhang / laterally cantilevered shapes;
- Usagi generalization;
- final artwork superiority / final artistic judgment.
